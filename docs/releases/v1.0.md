Super Mario War for Android, built on the current [Super Mario War](https://github.com/mmatyas/supermariowar) code, with full controller support, made for the AYN Odin 2 Portal and other Android handhelds, phones and TVs.

## Install

Download **SuperMarioWar-Android-v1.0.apk** below and open it on the device (allow installing from unknown sources if asked). Later versions install over it and keep your settings.

The `debug-symbols` file is only for turning crash reports into readable form; you don't need it to play.

## What's in it

**Controllers**
- Built-in controls of the AYN Odin 2 Portal and similar handhelds work out of the box
- Up to 4 players with built-in and Bluetooth/USB controllers: the first press claims the next player slot, external pads come before the built-in one, a reconnected pad keeps its slot, and holding **L + R** for a second makes that pad Player 1
- D-pad and analog stick work in every menu and in matches; holding a direction keeps scrolling in menus
- Every controller can drive the menus, team select, pause and the exit dialog
- On-screen touch controls for Player 1 when no controller is in use

**Display**
- Fullscreen landscape, GPU rendered
- CRT filters from RetroArch: **CRT Lottes**, **CRT EasyMode** and **CRT EasyMode Halation**, with **CRT Scanlines** at 240 (native) or 480
- **Menu Dimming** for easier-to-read menus
- Menu text fits its pipes everywhere (labels no longer overlap the rounded ends)

**Stability**
- No more missing menus on every other launch
- Fixed a crash when entering the main menu with a controller connected
- Several memory-safety and logic fixes; crash reports can be copied from the app after a crash

See [the Android guide](https://github.com/justthehazey-hash/supermariowar-android-2Pdualscreen/blob/master/docs/android.md) for controls and options.
